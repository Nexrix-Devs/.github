## 👋 What is Nexrix?

Nexrix is a community where young builders **learn, create, collaborate and innovate** together. We build real projects, share skills, and help each other grow — whether you're writing your first line of code or shipping your tenth app.

## 🤝 How to join

1. You're between **13 and 18** years old and curious about tech or design.
2. Follow us on any of our socials below and send us a message.
3. Join our meetups — online or in person in Tilottama, Rupandehi.
4. Start building with us! ⭐ Star and contribute to our repositories.
